a bathroom mirror for your webcam

Breath
on Glass

Fog up your own reflection, then write in it with a fingertip — the marks fade, the way they always did.

Nothing is uploaded. Your camera and microphone are processed right here in this browser tab. No frames, audio, or data ever leave your machine — the optional sound effects are synthesized locally too.

breathe · pinch · wipe

  • 01Breathe

    Blow softly at your laptop. Fog blooms right where your breath lands.

  • 02Pinch

    Touch thumb and index finger together and draw. The fog wipes away and your face shows through.

  • 03Fist

    Close your hand and swipe to clear a wide patch, like using your sleeve.

On a touchscreen, drag one finger to write, drag two fingers to wipe, and hold the breathe button to fog.
